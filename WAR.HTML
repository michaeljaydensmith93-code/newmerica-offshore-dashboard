What are war ranges?

Are you ready to start a war in Politics and War? Hold your horses! Not every nation is fair game for you to attack. The game has a system called War Ranges that automatically determines which nations you can declare war on.

But how does it work? It's simple: the game takes into account your nation's score and locks you into a range of only a select number of nations. Your score is calculated based on the number of cities, projects, and military units you have. The nations you can declare war on are those with a score within -25% to 150% of your score.

This system ensures that wars are fair and balanced, so people with massive empires can't bully smaller nations. But don't worry, there are still plenty of nations for you to take on and conquer within your range. So, before you lay siege to your next target, make sure you check your War Range and see who's fair game. Good luck, commander!

Alliance Departments

In most alliances, the government is split up into departments, each with its own unique responsibilities and focus. By working together, these departments help to keep the alliance running smoothly and ensure its success.

Foreign Affairs (FA) is often considered the most critical department. FA handles all communication with other alliances and is responsible for the alliance's diplomatic position, allies, involvement in wars, and public image. The head of foreign affairs is usually the most trusted person in the alliance after the leader.

Internal Affairs (IA) is responsible for organizing the alliance and making it a fun place to be. IA keeps track of members, processes new applications, and runs activities, events, and games outside of Politics and War.

Military Affairs (MA), also called Milcom or Defense, is responsible for the alliance's military organization. They coordinate wars and counters, educate members about the mechanics of wars, and supervise raiding.

Economic Affairs (EA), also known as Economics or Econ, is responsible for managing the alliance's finances. This includes member city builds, taxes, grants, loans, and keeping the alliance bank stable and growing while also growing the member nations.

These are just a few examples of the departments that most alliances have. By working together and utilizing each department's unique strengths, alliances can rise to the top and rule the world of Politics and War.

Displayed below are all of the wars your nation has been involved in for the past 30 days. You can view the statuses of your wars and execute attacks from this page. Military Action Points are used to execute military actions. One Military Action Point is generated per war every turn (2 hours). When a war is declared, both sides start with 6 Military Action Points. Different attacks cost different amounts of Military Action Points. Military Action Points will stack up to 12 points, at which point they will not stack further. To win a war, you must reduce your enemy's resistance to 0. Different attacks will reduce resistance by different amounts.
